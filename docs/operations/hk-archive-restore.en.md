Hong Kong market support was fully retired together with RQData on 2026-07-26. The former `marketdata migration freeze-hk` / `marketdata migration hydrate-hk` commands and `cold_storage.py` were removed from the active package and are no longer available.

For historical Hong Kong asset reproduction, use the `hk-freeze-20260613` tag or the private archive repository. Do not rely on a recovery entry point in this platform.
