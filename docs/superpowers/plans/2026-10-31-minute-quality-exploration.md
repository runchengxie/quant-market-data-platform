## Review focus

Unknown evidence must fail closed. Market scope must not be upgraded by changing
labels. Partial old candidates must not replace accepted ones. A changed quality
policy must change reader provenance. Earlier-history completion must use the
configured calendar range. No service may reference a temporary worktree.
